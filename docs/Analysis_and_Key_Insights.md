# Analysis and Key Insights

## Dataset

The supplied dataset contains 25 team records.

## Key findings

### 1. Highest Win %

Detroit Red Wings recorded the highest Win % at 75.6%, with 62 wins and a Goal Differential of +144.

### 2. Highest Goals For

Pittsburgh Penguins recorded the highest Goals For total at 362.

### 3. Lowest Win %

Ottawa Senators recorded the lowest Win % at 22.0%.

### 4. Lowest Goal Differential

San Jose Sharks recorded the lowest Goal Differential at -105.

### 5. Goal Differential and Win %

The correlation between Goal Differential and Win % is approximately **0.97**.

This indicates a very strong positive association in the supplied observations. It does not establish causation.

### 6. Goals For and Win %

The correlation between Goals For and Win % is approximately **0.69**, indicating a positive association.

### 7. Goals Against and Win %

The correlation between Goals Against and Win % is approximately **-0.72**, indicating that higher goals conceded are associated with lower Win % in this dataset.

## Analytical takeaway

The dashboard combines offensive and defensive measures rather than evaluating teams using Win % alone. Goal Differential provides a useful summary of the balance between scoring and conceding, while the scatter plot allows the user to examine its relationship with winning performance.

## Important interpretation

These findings describe the supplied dataset. They should not be presented as causal conclusions or general claims about hockey performance beyond the data.
